02 · Wellness

Zenvira

Inner peace, outer strength.

Zenvira is a private journaling companion. The name splits in two: Zen, for inner calm, and Vira, for the courage to keep going. It is explicitly not therapy and not a medical tool — a place to notice how you feel, then step away.

Friendly chat

A companion that listens. No advice, no diagnosis.

Daily check-in

Mood and energy, noticed gently over time.

Meditation player

Nature sound and guided audio, matched to time and mood.

Crisis support

A help button that points to people, not a chatbot counselor.

03 · Research software

GRAD

Geospatial, Remote Sensing, AI & Data Analytics.

GRAD is a workspace for research teams. I built it around one belief: the researcher does the research. AI can widen what a team can see. It does not make the call, and nothing enters a paper without a person approving it.

  1. IngestPapers, datasets, and notes become one shared knowledge base.
  2. InvestigateAsk questions. Gaps come back with sources attached.
  3. ExperimentRunSync writes a notebook, runs on your compute, and syncs the record.
  4. AuthorLive LaTeX, then domain-lead review before anything ships.

About

From the lab bench to something a person can open.

Specializing in building and deploying efficient, user-centric AI — from cloud to edge.

With an academic foundation in Electrical & Electronic Engineering from CUET and a Master’s degree from BUET, I am passionate about harnessing computer vision and AI for social impact. My journey began during undergraduate studies, where I developed an embedded health-monitoring system aimed at improving healthcare accessibility in underserved regions.

Graduate research at BUET focused on lightweight models for human activity detection. I developed a streamlined variant of YOLOv5, optimized for smaller objects, higher frames per second, and resource-constrained devices.

That same instinct now shows up in software I ship. Zenvira, GRAD, and The House Properties are live products. Avane Labs is the studio site for the practice behind them — useful, careful, and actually open.
